# Freeholds evidence tools

These offline tools measure checked-in content and print reviewable evidence. They
do not enable gameplay, publish balance approvals, or generate shipping artwork.
Keep observed measurements separate from proposed tuning and from owner approval.
Record source paths, hashes, units, derivation and reproducible fixture identities.

Do not couple independent economy and geometry producers merely to share a CLI.
Runtime modules must not import these tools or their unapproved proposal artifacts.
Parent instructions in `scripts/CLAUDE.md` own the normal tooling conventions.
