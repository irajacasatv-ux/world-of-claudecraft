Your Freehold

Walk through the gate on the Eastbrook quay

  • Your own place, shared by every character on your account. The Hearth Key returns you home on a shared cooldown, outside combat, death or jail.
  • The hearth is the heart of the house. Its flame tells you at a glance how well kept your home is. Day turns to night inside as it does outside.
  • The Inn Room is free for everyone: a bed and three plinths for your qualifying trophies.
  • Open house: private or friends first; guild and public visiting later. Choose a home at the gate.

Build it your way

  • Place owned furnishings within the room's limits. Floor placement first; wall, tabletop and fixed ceiling anchors later.
  • Later, move tables with their candles and use free planar placement. Undo/redo covers recent placement changes in this building session.
  • Mouse, gamepad and deliberate touch controls from the first release. Fixed structure stays fixed; no scaling or full-axis gimbal.

Upkeep

Keep the hearth lit

  • A home wants tending. The hearth shows its condition from the first release; richer finish effects follow later.
  • The Steward's Ledger lists produce and rotating everyday materials each week. Gather or buy them; review the exact bill and prepay four weeks, later twelve.
  • The Master Builder's Call is an optional repair purchase on supported web checkout. Its review shows whether it also pays the current bill.
Your home is never taken away. Let it go and the stations go quiet and the hearth goes out. Nothing is ever lost, and one ledger brings it all back.

Away for a while? Wear pauses after the absence window, with repair-free return days. Recorded market outages pause upkeep without catch-up bills.

Guildhalls

A door for every guild

  • Owned by the guild. Officers build; members manage assigned trophy plinths and keep their accomplishments when they leave.
  • The Hall Fund: members contribute within the shared allowance and read the ledger. Claudium contributions use supported checkout.
  • Build projects: meet shared material and fee requirements, then finish each project and display its trophy without an added waiting timer.

Reasons to gather

  • Feast night at the long table.
  • The war table: guild lockouts, then recorded first kills and their banners.
  • The muster board, the guild bank, shared crafting stations.
  • Cosmetic furnishing vendors unlocked by completed projects.

Our promises

What never changes

  1. The game never sells power. Housing is appearance, convenience, and access. Nothing you can buy changes a fight, a drop, or a level.
  2. Upkeep never repossesses. Home, trophies and furnishings stay safe; a voluntary sale transfers only its confirmed contents.
  3. Everyone gets a home entitlement. No land lottery or ownership waitlist; a busy instance may ask you to retry entry.
  4. Trophies come from your accomplishments, never a purchase. Known history counts across your characters.
  5. You do not need a profession to own, raise, or keep a house. Every material is on the market.
  6. The same useful information at every graphics setting. Supported online builds use your home; offline starts with a fresh Inn Room.
