Trophies

Everything you have conquered, on the wall

  • The Legend Stand: a Masterwrought piece you Perfected and named, displayed with your name for it and the maker's signature.
  • Mounted heads for the realm's rares. Statues for Thunzharr, Nythraxis, Ignivar and Varkhul, Korzul, Morthen, Vael, and Ysolei.
  • Armor stands and weapon racks that show the real item, piece by piece.
  • A paddock for your mounts, banners for your titles, and the Harvestmaster's golden sheaf for the garden.
Yours the moment you earn them. Everything you have already done counts from day one. Trophies are never sold.

Heroic and rift S-rank kills earn silver and gilded versions you can read from across the room. Inspect any trophy and it names the deed, the character, and the day it happened. Your visitors see the same.

Your Freehold

Walk through the gate on the Eastbrook quay

  • Your own place, shared by every character on your account. A Hearth Key walks you home from anywhere in the world.
  • The hearth is the heart of the house. Its flame tells you at a glance how well kept your home is. Day turns to night inside as it does outside.
  • The Inn Room is free for everyone: a bed, three trophy plinths, and your first mounted head.
  • Open house: keep it private, or open the door to friends, your guild, or the whole realm.

Build it your way

  • Pick up anything and place it where you like. Snap it to the floor, a wall, or a table top, or turn snapping off.
  • Move the table and the candles come with it. Undo anything.
  • Mouse, gamepad, and touch on day one. Beautiful on a phone.

Furnishings

Every craft builds the house

  • Weaponcrafting racks and iron fittings. Armorcrafting stands and braziers.
  • Tailoring rugs, curtains, and banners. Leatherworking hides and chairs.
  • Engineering lamps, clocks, and gadgets. Enchanting glow and enchanted light.
  • Inscription paintings, maps, and scrolls. Jewelcrafting chandeliers and gem lamps.
  • Alchemy dyes. Cooking feast tables. Farming the produce on them.

Blueprints

  • Learn new furnishings from blueprints found in raids, rifts, and delves, or bought from the quartermaster.
  • Crafters will have a whole new market: every piece placed is a piece somebody made.
  • Furnishings trade on the World Market for gold and on the $WOC marketplace.
Amenities

A house with a job

Strongbox

Your bank, at home. A Materials Vault chest as your house grows. Guildhalls get the guild bank.

Stations

Forge, kitchens, apothecary, tannery, loom, toolworks. In a Guildhall, every member present can use them.

Kitchen Garden

Your real farm plots, growing right outside the door, with the Harvest Journal on a board.

Feast hall

The long table where a cook lays out the party feast before a raid night.

Hearth

The Hearth Key brings you here from anywhere. A lit hearth means a well-kept home.

Never

No stat buffs, no bonus experience, no drop-rate bonuses. Your house is convenience and pride, never power.

Upkeep

Keep the hearth lit

  • A home wants tending. Dust settles, lanterns flicker, the hearth burns low.
  • The Steward's Ledger asks once a week for a few stacks of everyday materials: ore, wood, herbs, hides, cloth, fish, and produce from the farms. Gather them, buy them, or pay ahead for the season.
  • The Master Builder's Call fixes everything on the spot when you would rather not go gathering.
Your home is never taken away. Let it go and the stations go quiet and the hearth goes out. Nothing is ever lost, and one ledger brings it all back.

Away for a while? The house waits for you, and you get a few free days to settle back in.

Guildhalls

A door for every guild

  • Owned by the guild. Officers build; every member hangs their own trophies.
  • The Hall Fund: everyone chips in materials, gold, or Claudium, and everyone can see the ledger.
  • Build projects: raise the hall together, tier by tier, and hang a trophy for every one you finish.

Reasons to gather

  • Feast night at the long table.
  • The war table: lockouts, first kills, and first-kill banners on the wall.
  • The muster board, the guild bank, shared crafting stations.
  • Visiting vendors when a project completes.
Neighbors

Wards

Down the road: shared neighborhoods of freeholds around a square, with a Guildhall on the corner.

  • Walk the ward and see your neighbors' houses from the outside.
  • Monthly ward Endeavors everyone works on together, and rewards that grow everyone's home.
  • Seasonal Showcases: the realm votes on the finest houses, and the winners hang a trophy nobody else can.
  • Guest books at every door.
Built for $WOC

Every purchase settles in $WOC

  • Claim land, raise a tier, call the Master Builder: pay with Claudium however you like, and every purchase settles in $WOC behind the scenes. A share is burned. The treasury never sells.
  • Paying in $WOC is the cheapest way, as it already is in the store.
  • Every ledger pulls real materials off the World Market every week, so gatherers and farmers have a standing customer.

The Freehold Charter

  • Later on, your Freehold Charter can be minted on Solana as proof of your home, unique to your plot.
  • Charters will trade on the $WOC marketplace like any other treasure, with the same burn on every sale.
  • A charter is a key, not a coupon: no yield, no rent, no promises of anything but a home.
Our promises

What never changes

  1. The game never sells power. Housing is appearance, convenience, and access. Nothing you can buy changes a fight, a drop, or a level.
  2. Nothing is ever repossessed. Not your house, not your trophies, not your furniture.
  3. Everyone gets a plot. No lotteries, no land rushes, no waiting lists.
  4. Trophies are earned, never sold, and everything you have already done counts.
  5. You do not need a profession to own, raise, or keep a house. Every material is on the market.
  6. It works everywhere you play, on every device, at every graphics setting.
