## 1. WoW Midnight housing (2025 to 2026)

- Model: instanced neighborhoods of roughly 50 plots. Public ones are server-generated; Guild neighborhoods need 10 members active in the last 30 days (a second "layer" opens when 50 fill); Charter neighborhoods need 8 signatures and are invite-only for up to 50. Two houses per account (one Alliance zone, one Horde). Neighborhood Favor raises the decor limit for everyone in it. https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/player-housing-neighborhoods-guide
- Acquisition: click the plot sign, pay gold. Blizzard: "no exorbitant requirements or high purchase costs, no lotteries, and no onerous upkeep"; decor is Warband-wide. https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24244457/come-home-to-azeroth-with-housing-in-midnight Secondary guides put the plot at 1,000 gold with free moves (decor travels with you); I could not confirm the number from Blizzard. https://goombastomp.com/how-much-does-player-housing-really-cost-in-wow-midnight/
- Decor sourcing: achievements (retroactive, duplicates buyable from vendors), professions, vendors, quests, AH, raids. https://www.wowhead.com/guide/player-housing/midnight-decor-achievements Raid trophies: the final boss of each Season 1 raid drops Argent, Aureate, and Gleaming variants, apparently by difficulty; Gleaming was tied to Cutting Edge on PTR. https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/wow-midnight-season-1-raids-reward-trophy-housing-decor/
- Store: Hearthsteel is a real-money currency for housing items only; Blizzard says the "vast majority" of decor is earnable and gold can reach it via WoW Token. https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24242736/developer-insight-hearthsteel-virtual-currency-and-housing-in-midnight
- Editor: Basic and Advanced modes, free placement, dye, resize; moving an object moves what sits on it (an EQ2-style feel). https://tagn.wordpress.com/2026/03/18/wow-midnight-and-first-housing-impressions/ A dye station for Alchemists/Inscriptionists unlocks in the neighborhood town center as it levels. https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/player-housing-neighborhoods-guide
- Progression: 12 house levels via housing XP; interior decor budget 910 to 5,975 points, exterior 200 to 350, room budget 19 to 134, new room shapes and larger exteriors per level. https://housing.wowdb.com/progression/ Decor storage cap was 5,000 and is being raised to 7,500, which collectors still call inadequate (1,600+ unique pieces exist). https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/please-raise-decor-limit/2260866
- Endeavors: roughly monthly neighborhood-wide task sets, scaled to neighborhood size, unlocking themed NPCs and decor. https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24244457/come-home-to-azeroth-with-housing-in-midnight
- Upkeep: none. Blizzard also stated a lapsed subscription does not repossess a house. https://www.mmorpg.com/news/world-of-warcraft-breaks-down-its-vision-for-player-housing-and-thankfully-lapsed-subscriptions-dont-mean-repossession-2000134061
- Roadmap: layouts import/export, pets, visitor codes, guest books, voted showcases with trophy decor for winners, co-ownership, guild halls "soon after Midnight", 5 to 10 renown levels per patch. https://blizzardwatch.com/2026/03/20/upcoming-features-blizzard-announced-player-housing-though-theres-no-release-timeline/ https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/midnight-housing-update-details/
- Reception: GameSpot: "great, if you can afford the decor grind". https://www.gamespot.com/articles/wow-midnights-player-housing-is-great-if-you-can-afford-the-decor-grind/1100-6538925/ A widely read EU forum review (Dec 2025) called it "an in-game level designer" with no gameplay loop, roughly 100k gold for basic furniture, fence pieces placed one by one, vendor inventory spam. https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/housing-release-review/598121 TAGN: "there is no game reason to go to my house"; the starter-decor dump makes items feel "less special". Engagement claim: 4 million hours and 14 million decor placed in the first two weeks of early access (search summary only; page fetch blocked). https://wccftech.com/world-of-warcraft-midnight-roadmap-housing-blizzcon-2026/ The claim that tier-3 houses add a 50-slot bank vault comes only from boosting sites (e.g. https://www.parsepal.gg/guides/player-housing-guide); unverified.

## 2. FFXIV housing

- Plots: small 3M to 3.75M gil, medium 16M to 20M, large 40M to 50M. Lottery runs a 9-day cycle (5-day entry, 4-day results); an unclaimed win forfeits 50% of the deposit. Furnishing caps: 300/40, 450/60, 600/80 (interior/exterior); apartments 150, cost 500k, never demolished, no gardening. FC estates need rank 6 and 4 members with 30 days tenure; private chambers 300k gil each. https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing
- Scale: 30 wards per district gives 9,000 plots and 27,000 apartments per server (900 large, 2,100 medium, 6,000 small); first 18 wards are FC-only. Empyreum's 1,440 plots had only 360 private. https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/12871-final-fantasy-xivs-housing-situation-is-still-a-depressing-mess-and-it-deserves-better (June 2022)
- Demolition: warnings at day 30, 35, 42; demolition at 45 days without the owner (or any FC member) physically entering; 80% of plot price refunded, 35 days to reclaim furnishings; fixtures, private chambers, workshop and airships are lost. https://www.destructoid.com/ffxiv-housing-demolition-and-relocation-explained/ (May 2024)
- Controversy: demolition was suspended for 2+ years around COVID/Endwalker (RPG Site), and again on EU from Nov 7, 2024 (Valencia floods) for nine months; when 7.3 resumed it in Aug 2025, inactive timers resumed from November so some hit zero instantly, producing "Thousands of FFXIV Players Just Lost Their House" videos. https://wowcarry.com/blog/final-fantasy-xiv/news/ffxiv-drama-thousands-lose-houses-amid-xeno-controversy RPG Site's core complaint: housing "forces you to stay subscribed and actively playing".
- Guild projects: the workshop needs an FC house (size irrelevant); airships are 4 parts (hull, rigging, forecastle, aftcastle) x 3 phases of crafted materials, deploy on ceruleum tanks (100 FC credits each, FC rank 6), 24 sectors, parts degrade and need Magitek repair materials; 1/3/5/7 flight credits for airships 1 to 4. https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Free_Company_Airships Workshop addition 800,000 gil and first flight credit 10,000 FC credits (2015 source). https://aywren.com/2015/06/23/ffxiv-heavensward-free-company-airship-guide/
- Gil sinks: plot price (20% burned on demolition via the 80% refund), 500k apartments, 300k private chambers, 800k workshop, furnishings on the market board (no volume figures found).
- Loved: deep decorating, FC social hubs, workshops as a shared guild project. Hated: scarcity, subscription coercion, demolition losing workshops, FC wards sitting empty while private wards are full.

## 3. ESO homes

- Purchase: gold or crowns for most homes: apartments 11k to 13k gold (600 to 660 crowns), small 40k to 73k (2,100 to 2,600 crowns), medium 190k to 335k (3,500 to 4,400), large 760k to 1.3M (5,500 to 7,200), manors 1M to 3.8M gold (7,000 to 15,000 crowns); a free inn room via the "Room to Spare" quest, more inn rooms at 3,000 gold. Furnishings crafted via furnishing plans across five tradeskills (woodworking, blacksmithing, clothing, alchemy, enchanting); achievement furnishers, home goods vendors, luxury furnisher, Crown Store, fishing drops, veteran dungeon Undaunted busts. No upkeep. https://elderscrollsonline.wiki.fextralife.com/Player+Housing
- Caps (Update 49, Mar 9, 2026, first raise since 2017): Staple 75 traditional / 2 special / 10 collectibles / 2 special collectibles; Classic 250 / 4 / 25 / 4; Notable unchanged at 350 / 5 / 55; ESO Plus doubles every category; visitor caps 6/12/24; 32 homes recategorized upward, prices unchanged. https://hacktheminotaur.com/eso-guides/eso-update-49/ https://eso-hub.com/en/news/eso-housing-slots-increase-up-to-900-in-update-49
- Guild halls are just large homes: grand master crafting stations, mundus stones, target dummies, transmute station; the 24-visitor cap (12 without ESO Plus) is the real constraint on guild events. https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/652578/what-size-houses-for-guild-halls (Feb 2024)
- Take-away: cosmetic monetization coexists with a gold path to almost every home; the P2W-adjacent sore point is the subscriber slot doubling, and caps stayed frozen for nine years.

## 4. RuneScape / OSRS player-owned house

- 1,000 coins to buy (or free via the Daddy's Home miniquest); 24 rooms on a 3x3 grid at level 1, +1 room every 6 levels from 26, 38 rooms on 7x7 at 99; relocation 5k to 50k coins. Gilded altar (75 Construction) gives 350% prayer XP with burners lit; ornate rejuvenation pool (90) restores HP, prayer, special, run, cures poison; ornate jewellery box (91) is unlimited teleports; portal chamber/nexus give cross-spellbook teleports. https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Player-owned_house https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Portal_nexus
- Servants: Rick (lvl 20, 500 coins hire, 63 coins per 8 uses, 6-item trips) to Demon butler (50, 10,000, 1,250, 26 items); need two bedrooms with beds; fetch from bank, un-note, run the sawmill. https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Servant
- Costume room (42 Construction, 50,000 gp): armour case (25 sets at oak to unlimited at mahogany 82), cape rack (up to 10 accomplishment capes at gilded 81), magic wardrobe, toy box, treasure chest, fancy dress box; the achievement gallery holds mounted heads and trophies. It exists to free bank space. https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Costume_room
- Why beloved: every room is a utility (restore, pray, teleport, store), and the skill gate makes the house a progression trophy in itself; efficient PvM loops route through the house. https://tonsofxp.com/blog/osrs-poh-guide https://www.theoatrix.net/post/osrs-max-house-guide

## 5. Guild-house exemplars

- EQ2: T1 (guild lvl 30) 25 plat, upkeep 25 gold + 50,000 status/week; T2 (50) 100 plat, 1 plat + 100k status; T3 (70) 500 plat, 5 plat + 200k; T4 (95) 1,000 plat, 10 plat + 400k/week; amenity slots 5 at lvl 30 up to 37 at 95; members deposit coin/status into a non-refundable escrow; upkeep prepayable; unpaid upkeep locks members out of the hall. https://eq2.fanbyte.com/wiki/Guild_Halls_(EQ2) https://eq2.fandom.com/wiki/Guild_Halls https://eq2.fandom.com/wiki/Guild_Hall_Amenities
- Lineage 2: clan halls are auctioned; weekly rent e.g. 1,000,000 adena in Aden, 500,000 in Gludio/Gludin; one week grace then repossessed and re-auctioned. https://www.l2scroll.com/2015/10/clan-halls.html https://legacylineage2.fandom.com/wiki/Updates_-_C1_-_Clan_Halls
- BDO: biweekly auction (only guild masters bid, 3 bids); daily maintenance is 5% of the winning bid (a 100M bid costs 5M silver per day), auto-deducted from guild funds. https://www.naeu.playblackdesert.com/en-US/Wiki?wikiNo=169
- WildStar: plot at level 14 (cozy 1 gold, spacious 3 plat); six sockets (one 2x2, two 1x2, four 1x1) take plugs: crafting stations, banks, vendors, buff statues, gathering nodes, gardens, 30-minute challenges rewarding decor/renown/dyes, scalable mini-dungeons, biome portals; all built from FABKits; editor with numeric XYZ, Euler angles, scale, copy/paste, and parent-child linking; housing grants rest XP (50% bonus) and you can log out at a neighbor's plot for theirs; roommates share harvest rights. https://thefanaticalswordsman.com/2014/06/27/guide-to-wildstars-housing/ Still ranked top-three housing ever because it was "a base of operations". https://massivelyop.com/2019/12/15/massively-ops-2019-awards-best-mmo-housing/ https://massivelyop.com/2018/07/25/the-daily-grind-rank-the-top-three-housing-mmos-ever-made/
- SWG: city ranks by citizens: Outpost 5 (city hall), Village 10 (bank, garage, cantina), Township 15 (cloning, hospital), City 30 (shuttleport, theater), Metropolis 40; mayor elections every 3 weeks; five tax types (property, income, sales, travel, garage); weekly city updates charge maintenance; losing citizens downgrades rank and destroys rank-gated structures; caps of 50 cities on Dantooine/Lok/Talus/Rori and 20 on Naboo/Tatooine/Corellia. https://www.mmorpg.com/guides/city-building-guide-2000116198 What made it magical: emergent malls, cantinas, design competitions, role-play towns. What broke: one-man cities filled the caps, merchants built shanty towns around NPC starports, no terrain leveling or roads, ghost towns after the NGE. https://massivelyop.com/2016/01/20/working-as-intended-what-star-wars-galaxies-got-wrong/ https://www.ruinnation.com/news/182-player-cities-and-housing
- UO: one house per account and a 7-day placement cooldown. https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/gameplay/houses-placing-a-house/ Renaissance (May 2000) doubled the land with Trammel largely to relieve housing pressure; Malas later added 1,500 more plots. https://www.uoguide.com/Ultima_Online:_Renaissance Publish 16 made the primary house auto-refresh while the account is active and condemned any other houses on the account. https://www.uoguide.com/Publish_16_-_Housing_Ownership_Changes Official servers give a 90-day inactivity grace (re-enabled 2012), then six decay stages over 3 to 5 days, then IDOC drops at 5/10/15 h with contents packed into barrels breakable only unarmed. https://uo.com/2012/09/10/update-on-housing-decay/ https://www.uoguide.com/House_Decay https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/gameplay/houses-placing-a-house/condemned-houses-idoc/ IDOC hunters scripted the timers for a decade. https://www.uoforum.com/threads/official-change-to-house-decay.36522/

## 6. Upkeep and taxes as a design lever

- New World: houses 5k/10k/15k/20k coin at territory standing 10/15/20/30; weekly tax 400 to 1,600 base (a 2023 guide lists 250/500/750/1,000 in low-tax towns), governor sets 5 to 20% (default 10%), standing cards cut 15 to 20%; first house 50% off price and tax; unpaid tax disables redecorating, trophy bonuses, extra storage, and fast travel but never the house; 3 houses max, 5 trophies each. https://www.thegamer.com/new-world-housing-property-tax-furniture-guide/ https://www.gamewatcher.com/news/new-world-house-tax Amazon cut taxes 90% for two weeks in Nov 2021 as exploit compensation. https://www.mmorpg.com/news/new-world-applies-fix-for-time-skip-bug-reduces-housing-tax-by-90-until-december-2000123708 The game's closure was announced Oct 28, 2025 (search summary, not fetched). https://newworld.fandom.com/wiki/Housing
- ArcheAge: 5 to 50 tax certificates per property per week; owning 3 properties costs 100% extra, 8 to 10 costs 500% (v3.0), account-wide, explicitly to punish hoarding; maxed Construction can craft unbound certificates to sell. https://archeage.fandom.com/wiki/Tax_Certificate https://na.archerage.to/forums/threads/3-0-updates-land-and-taxation.4962/ Unpaid land drops and gets sniped, historically by click-bots. https://www.mmobomb.com/news/land-grab-hacks-add-archeage-woes $50 to $150 head-start packs turned the launch land rush into pay-to-win. https://www.engadget.com/2014-09-19-working-as-intended-lessons-from-the-history-of-mmo-housing.html (2014)
- EVE: service modules burn fuel blocks, not the hull; with none online the structure is "low power" (easier to kill), and after 7 days (+0 to 24 h) with no fuel consumed it is "abandoned": no reinforcement, no tethering, contents drop as loot instead of asset safety. Onlining a service restores full power. https://support.eveonline.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014282739-Upwell-Structures-Abandoned-State Asset safety otherwise charges 0.5% (same system after 5 days) or 15% (after a 20-day auto-move). https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Asset_safety
- Albion: personal island upgrades 18,750 to 3,750,000 silver (6.45M total) for 11 regular + 2 small + 5 farm plots at L6; guild island 137,500 to 18,225,000 (27.46M total) with guild hall, guild bank, marketplace, stadium; laborers return every 22 h; premium halves costs; no upkeep; moving means abandoning everything. https://altarofgaming.com/albion-online-personal-guild-island-guide/ https://onelifegaming.com/en/albion-online/blog/island-guide
- Entropia: 60,000 Calypso Land Deeds at 1,000 PED ($100) each, each paying 1/60,000 of 50% of planet partner gross revenue weekly, audited semi-annually; deeds trade freely and were later converted to exchange shares. https://www.entropiauniverse.com/bulletin/buzz/2011/11/16/Calypso-Land-Lot-Deeds.xml http://entropiaplanets.com/wiki/Calypso_Land_Deeds
- Pax Dei: monthly plot tokens; tying plots to the subscription drew enough backlash that an in-game Grace path was added in late 2025. https://massivelyop.com/2025/11/05/pax-dei-is-finally-planning-to-let-players-acquire-a-housing-plot-without-a-subscription/
- Patterns: tolerated = small weekly fees that only gate perks (New World lockout, EQ2 lockout), prepayable, with a grace week (L2). Rage = anything that destroys the house or its contents on a timer (FFXIV 45 days, UO IDOC, ArcheAge land drops, EVE abandoned loot), especially after suspensions resume. The 2016 "11 commandments" list "no weekly or monthly upkeep tolls" outright. https://www.mmogypsy.com/2016/09/11-commandments-great-mmo-player-housing.html

## 7. Cross-cutting lessons

- Retention data: no rigorous public study of housing retention lift exists. The only numbers are WoW's first-two-weeks engagement (above, unverified) and RPG Site's observation that FFXIV housing keeps subscriptions alive by threat. A 2014 survey of housing history found instanced housing without hooks into the world (Garrisons, LOTRO) goes dead. https://www.engadget.com/2014-09-19-working-as-intended-lessons-from-the-history-of-mmo-housing.html LOTRO players describe neighborhoods as "ghost towns". https://forums-old.lotro.com/forums/showthread.php?358590-Time-to-resell-all-those-empty-houses-end-the-ghost-towns-they-are-depressing
- Visitation: WoW is bolting on visitor codes, guest books, and voted showcases because early reviews said there is no reason to visit; WildStar's rest-XP-at-a-neighbor's is the best "reason to visit" mechanic on record; ESO's 6/12/24 visitor cap is what actually limits guild events.
- Decor economies: ESO routes furnishings through all five crafting skills plus achievement vendors; FFXIV furnishings are a standing market-board category; WoW added a profession dye station. No published volume figures found.
- Failure modes: empty instanced housing (Garrisons, LOTRO, early WoW reviews); land scarcity griefing (UO IDOC scripts, ArcheAge click-bots, SWG one-man cities); P2W creep (ArcheAge packs, ESO Plus slot doubling, Hearthsteel suspicion); editor jank (hook systems, fence-by-fence placement); frozen caps (ESO 2017 to 2026; WoW 5,000 storage); performance caps (Palia 3,000 to 3,500 items, WoW disabling exterior lights). https://palia.com/news/patch-166

## Top 12 lessons for a small classic-style MMO

1. Never destroy a house or its contents on a timer. Lock perks on missed upkeep (New World, EQ2), keep the shell and storage, and auto-restore on payment. Every demolition system on record produced the worst press.
2. Give the house a job. OSRS and WildStar are beloved because you go there to do things (restore, teleport, bank, craft); WoW's biggest complaint is "no reason to go home".
3. Upkeep should be cheap, weekly, and prepayable, not daily. Daily material upkeep compounds the FFXIV "must log in" resentment; BDO's 5%/day is only tolerated because guilds pool it. Add a grace window before lockout.
4. No land scarcity. Instanced plots for everyone (WoW, ESO, OSRS); never first-come plots or lotteries.
5. Trophies must be earned in content and tiered by difficulty (WoW Argent/Aureate/Gleaming, OSRS mounted heads, New World trophies), and shown with provenance so the house reads as a record of feats, not an IKEA catalog.
6. Cap by point budget, then raise it every patch. Announce the raise cadence; frozen caps are a nine-year grievance in ESO. (Superseded 2026-09-06 by D69, original retained as the dated trail. Adopted: review point-budget headroom on a published cadence; WOC proposes a review every second release, with increases only when LOW evidence supports them; no automatic rise is promised.)
7. Free placement with stacking and linking (EQ2/WildStar/Rift lineage) beats hook grids; ship bulk move and layout save/load early, WoW is retrofitting both. (Superseded 2026-09-06 by D43, D44 and D66, original retained as the dated trail. Adopted: placement tools are comparative precedents; WOC proposes initial floor placement and bounded placement undo/redo, later planar/yaw freedom with typed surfaces and fixed ceiling anchors, and later layout sharing. Scale, full-axis gimbal and collision leniency are outside the packet.)
8. Route decor through every profession and vendor rep you already have, and let tiers of decor be crafted, not bought.
9. Build the reason to visit: a neighbor bonus (WildStar rest XP), guest books, timed showcases with trophy rewards. (Superseded 2026-09-06 by D56, D59 and D60, original retained as the dated trail. Adopted: give guests social reasons to visit; WOC excludes new rest-XP or other power bonuses; later opt-in realm Showcases and moderated reaction books provide cosmetic/social goals with separate plot-entry privacy.)
10. Guild halls should be a shared plot with pooled escrow and amenity slots that scale with guild level (EQ2), never an auction (L2/BDO) on one realm.
11. Keep the store cosmetic-only and keep a gold path to everything (ESO gold prices, Blizzard's "vast majority earnable" line); never sell caps, slots, or upkeep relief. (Superseded 2026-09-06 by D29 and D30 under ruling 5, original retained as the dated trail. Adopted: the WOC scope sells cosmetic convenience/access on approved checkout surfaces, including tiers and optional repair; trophies remain free, inputs tradable, and no purchase grants combat or progression power.)
12. Budget for performance from day one: per-plot decor counts, exterior light limits, and visitor caps are the numbers every studio ended up tuning under pressure.
