## 1. Reliquary and Book of Deeds

- Reliquary page shape: `src/sim/content/reliquary.ts:ReliquaryPageDef` = {id, shelf ('conquerors'|'professions'|'horizons'), name, desc?, clearSource? (dungeon | delve | deed_stat | none), secondaryClearSource?, excludeFromCompletion? ('retired'|'personal'), sourceDefault?, relics}. A relic is `ReliquaryRelicDef`, a union of {kind:'item', itemId} | {kind:'mark', markId} | {kind:'mount', mountId} | {kind:'weapon_skin', skinId} | {kind:'title', deedId}, each with an optional `source` (`ReliquarySourceHint` {sourceKind, sourceId}; kinds boss, zone, profession, deed, vendor, delve, rift, quest, store, activity). Tables and projections: `RELIQUARY_PAGES`, `RELIQUARY_PAGE_ORDER` (append-only), `RELIQUARY_PAGES_BY_ID`, `RELIQUARY_ITEM_TO_PAGES`, `RELIQUARY_MARK_IDS`, `RELIQUARY_MARK_TO_PAGES`, `isCataloguedRelicItem`, `isCataloguedRelicMark`, `reliquaryRelicSource`, `RELIQUARY_HEROIC_GEAR`, `RELIQUARY_SET_MEMBERS`, `RELIQUARY_HORIZON_MOUNTS`, `RELIQUARY_HORIZON_WEAPON_SKINS`, `RELIQUARY_HORIZON_TITLES`, `RELIQUARY_PROFESSION_MARKS`, `RELIQUARY_PROFESSION_SPECIMEN_ITEMS`.
- What a player "owns" once conquered: nothing physical. Item relics are owned through `deedStats.itemsDiscovered`, fed by the first-obtain hub `src/sim/deeds.ts:markItemDiscovered` (credited only at grant through addItem / addItemInstance, never at corpse roll). Marks live in `reliquary.marks` on the PlayerMeta reliquary blob; mounts by possession of reins in bags or bank; weapon skins by account cosmetics; titles by `deedsEarned`. Sticky per-character records: `illuminatedPages` (first-ever page completion), per-relic obtain counts folded onto the first-find entry (`src/sim/reliquary.ts:reliquaryWireJson`, memoized). Curator ranks at 1 / 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 owned (apprentice, keeper, master, grand, eternal in `src/sim/reliquary.ts`). Rule 1 (`docs/design/reliquary.md`): cosmetic only, never power, convenience, drop rate, or actionable combat information. The doc's "Deliberately deferred" list includes "Housing museum props (no housing system yet)", so trophies are an anticipated extension, and the locked decisions include "no weapon-skin reward at rank 5" and "retro (join catch-up) is silent".
- Performance contract to inherit: no per-drop character save (fills ride the 30 s autosave), sparse omit-empty blob fields, wire thrift (id-only `reliquaryUnlock`, memoized blob), catalog growth is the bound. Character autosave rewrites the full JSONB every `AUTOSAVE_SECONDS` (30, `server/game.ts`).
- Deed shape: `src/sim/types.ts:DeedDef` = {id, name, desc, category, renown 0|5|10|25|50, trigger: DeedTrigger, reward?: DeedReward, hidden?, feat?}. `DeedReward` = {kind:'title', text} | {kind:'border', slug}. `DeedTrigger` is a closed union: level, lifetimeXp, quest / quests, stat (over `DeedStatKey`), dungeonClears, delveClears, arenaRating, craftSkill, gathering, collectItems, visit / visits, meta, meter, flag, manual. Counters: `src/sim/types.ts:DEED_STAT_KEYS` (kills, deaths, damageDealt, crits, lootCopper, tradesCompleted, mailAttachmentsSent, craftsPerformed, guildsFounded, marketSaleCopper, dungeonFinalBossKills, thunzharrKills, masterworksCrafted, salvagesPerformed, riftClears, riftSRankClears, tutorialGraduations, and more). `DeedStats` = {counters, itemsDiscovered: Set, visited: Set, dungeonClears}. New counters are bumped through the SimContext callback `bumpDeedStat`; a state change without a counter calls `markDeedsDirty`. Rules that bind every deed (`docs/design/deeds.md`): cosmetic only; zero Renown for luck; closed trigger vocabulary; never retro-edit a trigger; count outcomes not attempts; no permanently missable deeds. Recipe: append the `DeedDef` at the END of `DEEDS` in `src/sim/content/deeds.ts`; pins in `tests/deeds_content.test.ts` and `tests/deeds_sites_pin.test.ts`.
- Persistence: authority is the character blob (`PlayerMeta.deedsEarned` Map, `activeTitle`, `activeBorder`, `src/sim/sim.ts:1688`). The server is an observer: `server/deeds_db.ts:insertCharacterDeed` / `insertCharacterDeeds` upsert into `character_deeds` (realm, character_id, account_id, deed_id) with ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING; `recentDeedsForCharacter`, `earnedDeedIdsForAccount` (feeds Steam and Epic mirrors), `getDeedBroadcasts` / `setDeedBroadcasts` (the `accounts.deed_broadcasts` consent flag). Reliquary has NO table: `server/reliquary_rarity_db.ts:reliquaryRarityCounts` unnests the JSONB paths `$.deedStats.itemsDiscovered`, `$.reliquary.marks`, `$.reliquary.illuminatedPages` under `RELIQUARY_RARITY_STATEMENT_TIMEOUT_MS` (10 s).

## 3. Item durability, repair, and time-based loops

- No item durability and no repair anywhere. `src/sim/types.ts:877` ("this repo has no durability mechanic anywhere"), `src/sim/professions/tools.ts:8`, `src/sim/content/items.ts:648`. Death charges nothing (`src/sim/tutorial/death_lesson.ts:13`). There is no repair NPC and no repair cost formula. The only "durability" is tool-effect charges: `slot.durability` / `slot.maxDurability` on a tool-effect slot (`src/sim/professions/tools.ts`, `startingDurabilityFor`, `resolveSlotToolEffect`), decremented deterministically only when the bonus changed the outcome (`gathering.ts:904`), recharged by a cast-paced command priced in the arcane material (`tool_effect_actions.ts`).
- Daily boundary precedent (server-authoritative, sim-deterministic): the server computes `server/raid_reset.ts:resetDayKey(nowMs, zone)` (03:00 in the realm's IANA zone, `RAID_RESET_HOUR` 3, `DEFAULT_RAID_RESET_TIME_ZONE` America/New_York, `nextRaidResetMs`, weekly `nextWeeklyRaidResetMs` on Tuesday `WEEKLY_RESET_WEEKDAY` 2, `eventLeadDayKey`; pure in (instant, zone), memoized per epoch minute) and feeds `Sim.resetDay` and `Sim.eventLeadDay` each tick (`src/sim/sim.ts:1930`, exposed as `ctx.resetDay` / `ctx.eventLeadDay` in `src/sim/sim_context.ts:276`). Consumers compare a persisted date string and roll over: `src/sim/pvp/honor.ts:203` (`if (ctx.resetDay && daily.date !== ctx.resetDay)`), `src/sim/delves/runs.ts:392` (the delve daily), `src/sim/social/battleground.ts:2207` (first-win bonus), `src/sim/pvp/honor_event.ts:doubleHonorActive`. Empty key means "no calendar known" (headless and replay stay reproducible). Raid lockouts: the host injects `SimContext.raidResetMs`; the sim stores the number in `meta.raidLockouts` (world boss uses `WORLD_BOSS_LOCKOUT_PREFIX`).
- Continuous accrual and decay precedents: rested XP `src/sim/progression/xp.ts:updateRested` (per tick DT; 5 percent of the level's XP per 8 in-game hours at 60 sim-seconds per hour, `RESTED_FILL_FRACTION`, `RESTED_FILL_HOURS`, cap 1.5 levels `RESTED_CAP_LEVELS`); mail expiry in sim-seconds with an id-staggered refresh (`PostOffice.update`, `MAIL_PERSIST_REFRESH_SECONDS` 3600, `MAIL_EXPIRY_SECONDS` 14 days, `MAIL_ATTACHMENT_EXPIRY_SECONDS` 30 days); node respawn persisted as remaining-time deltas (`CharacterState.nodeHarvestCooldowns`); world boss `WORLD_BOSS_INTERVAL_SECONDS` 3600 sim-seconds; focus respec `durationMsPerPoint` on the lockout clock. Daily rewards (`server/daily_rewards.ts`, `server/daily_reward_schedule.ts:DailyRewardScheduleCache`) are account-level, wall-clock, server-only, with the offline stub `src/sim/daily_rewards_stub.ts:dailyRewardsStub` (the one sanctioned $WOC vocabulary file inside `src/sim`, guarded by `tests/architecture.test.ts`).

## 4. Items and rarity

- `src/sim/types.ts:ItemDef` = ArmorItemDef | WeaponItemDef | JewelryItemDef | HeldOffhandItemDef | OtherItemDef | MountItemDef. `MountItemDef` = {kind:'mount', mount: MountKey}. `OtherItemDef.kind` = every `ItemKind` except armor, weapon, held_offhand, mount. `ItemKind` = weapon, armor, held_offhand, quest, junk, food, drink, tool, potion, elixir, bag, mount. Quality: `'poor' | 'common' | 'uncommon' | 'rare' | 'epic' | 'legendary'`; colors `src/ui/icons.ts:QUALITY_COLOR` = poor #9d9d9d, common #ffffff, uncommon #1eff00, rare #0070dd, epic #a335ee, legendary #ff8000 (CSS token fallback `--color-quality-default`).
- `ItemUse` union: {type:'fishing'}, {type:'throw'}, {type:'summon'}, {type:'passingStone'}, {type:'mechChroma', chromaId}, {type:'skinSelect', catalog?}, {type:'gatherTool', professionId, tier}, {type:'toolEffect', effectId}. Def-level flags on `BaseItemDef`: sellValue (required, copper), buyValue, priceHonor, noVendorSell, noDiscard, noMarketList, soulbound (enforced in social/trade.ts, mail/post_office.ts, market.ts, items.ts), teachesRiding, stackSize (default weapon/armor/bag/tool 1, else 20 via `stackSizeOf`), requiredLevel (derived from quality via `src/sim/item_level_req.ts` when omitted), set, heroicOf, heroic, materialsOnly, bagSlots. Instance-level flags on `ItemInstancePayload` (types.ts:1230+): signer, charges, rolled {quality, stats, masterwork}, enchant, craftedRecipeId, boundTo, bindOnTrade (arms bind on first trade), locked (player safety mark), partyTrade, rift.
- Adding a kind (say 'furniture' or 'blueprint'): extend `ItemKind`; `src/sim/inventory_sort.ts:KIND_RANK` (typed `Record<ItemKind, number>`) and `src/ui/item_kind_label.ts:ITEM_KIND_LABEL_KEYS` (typed `Record<ItemDef['kind'], TranslationKey>`) fail to compile until filled; add the `itemUi.kind.<kind>` key to the matching `src/ui/i18n.catalog/` module; `OtherItemDef` covers the new kind automatically; records go in a `src/sim/content/<domain>.ts` table merged by `data.ts` (never inline in sim.ts). Precedent for "using an item grants a durable unlock": `use.type 'mechChroma'` items named `<chromaId>_armor_plate` (`src/sim/content/skins.ts:mechChromaItemId`).
- Art obligation (`src/sim/content/CLAUDE.md:159`): every non-heroic item needs one exactly named `public/ui/items/<item-id>.webp` and a provenance owner entry in `public/ui/items/mapping.json`, following `docs/design/item-icon-art-style.md` (`woc-item-icon-v1`); plus its English name in `src/ui/i18n.catalog/items.ts` and non-Latin name fills when wordy (M16). About 1,040 WebPs ship today.

## 5. Mounts and account cosmetics as ownership precedents

- Mount ownership is possession, per character: a mount is owned while its reins item (kind 'mount', `mount` = MountKey) sits in bags or bank; reins are NOT soulbound and travel by trade, mail, or market (`src/sim/mounts.ts:mountItemId`, `ownedMounts`). No persisted selected mount: using the reins summons (`summonMountItem`); live state is `Entity.mountKey` wired like `skin`. Ownership re-validated every `MOUNT_OWNERSHIP_REVALIDATE_TICKS` (4). The single gate is `ridingTrained` on PlayerMeta, bought from Marla for `RIDING_SKILL_FEE_COPPER` 800_000 (80 g, `src/sim/mounts_training.ts`); Valorsteed reins `buyValue` 100_000 (10 g). `MountDef` {key, name, rarity, moveSpeedPct}.
- Account cosmetics are per ACCOUNT: `accounts.cosmetics JSONB` (`server/db.ts:497` at this revision, the `ALTER TABLE accounts ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS cosmetics JSONB` line; the captured `:488` had rotted, plus `weapon_skin_ids` / `weapon_skin_loadout` columns), normalized by `server/db.ts:normalizeAccountCosmetics` into `AccountCosmetics` {completedQuestIds, mechChromaIds, weaponSkinIds, weaponSkinLoadout}; writes via `server/db.ts:addAccountCosmeticId`, `grantAccountWeaponSkins`; live merge `server/game.ts:updateLiveAccountCosmetics` (3536), `grantWeaponSkinsToAccount`. Wire: the `cosmetics` field on the `self` heavy record, decoded by `src/net/account_cosmetics_wire.ts:normalizeAccountCosmetics` (drops malformed entries) into `IWorldCosmetics.accountCosmetics` (`src/world_api/cosmetics.ts`: `changeSkin`, `claimEventSkin`, `unequipMechChroma`, weapon skin apply). Offline `Sim` enforces type match only; the paid store is online-only.
- Paid rail: Claudium (server-authoritative soft currency) proxied by `server/claudium.ts` to the economy service (`server/claudium_proxy.ts`, fails closed). SKU allowlists are data-as-code with no prices: `src/sim/content/weapon_skins.ts:WeaponSkinDef` (kind 'skin') and `src/sim/content/storage_charters.ts:STORAGE_SKUS` (kind 'storage', `isKnownStorageSkuId`). The idempotent purchase-to-sim-grant flow is `server/storage_purchases.ts` (live session required, per-character mutex, durable pending row in `server/storage_purchase_db.ts`, client-minted idempotency key, receipt key stored inside the character blob, one-transaction apply, resume at next login). $WOC holder tier is cosmetic flair on the identity wire (`server/game.ts:1433`, key `ht`, refreshed on an interval). Cosmetic commands are throttled by `server/cosmetic_op_guard.ts` (burst 10, refill 1/s) because identity-field changes re-wire the full identity record to everyone in range.

## 6. Mail and the Ravenpost

- `src/sim/mail/post_office.ts:PostOffice` is a SimContext module owning the world-scoped mail book, keyed by stable recipient identity (character id string online, entity id offline). Persisted as a per-realm JSONB world_state row (`server/mail_db.ts`, partitioned; `serializeMail`, `loadMail`, `takeDirtyMailPartitions`, `markPartitionsDirty`). Draws no rng. `MailKind` = player | system | npc.
- System delivery APIs: `sendLetter(recipientKey, recipientName, letter: LetterDef, kind)` books an authored fungible parcel (`src/sim/content/letters.ts:LetterDef` {senderName, letterId, subject, body, copper?, items?, delaySeconds?}; default NPC delay 90 s). `mailSystemParcel(recipient {key, name}, letter, items: InvSlot[], custodyRef?)` delivers EXACT item copies with instance payloads intact, no proximity, no postage, never refused, recipient may be offline, Infinity expiry while attachments remain, and dedupes on `custodyRef` (`hasCustodyParcel`), the $WOC marketplace escrow precedent (`server/mail_custody_overlay.ts`). Existing callers: `sendWelcome`, `mailHeroicMarks`, `queueQuestLetter`. Take path `mailTake` grants through the inventory hub (bag capacity checked, `canGrantCopies`).
- Constants: `MAIL_POSTAGE` 30 copper, `MAIL_MAX_ATTACHMENTS` 3, `MAIL_DELIVERY_SECONDS` 45, `MAIL_ATTACHMENT_EXPIRY_SECONDS` 30 days (one return-to-sender cycle; system mail exempt), `MAIL_MAX_PER_RECIPIENT` 100, `MAIL_SUBJECT_MAX` 64, `MAIL_BODY_MAX` 600. `mailKeyFor(meta)` resolves the recipient key; `rekeyMailOwner` / `purgeMailOwner` handle rename and deletion.
