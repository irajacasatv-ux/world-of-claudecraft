# Part 5: the four rulings of 2026-09-29, and what executing them measured

Asked in one question set at the session's start, each with a recommendation; all four
answered as recommended. The question and the chosen option, verbatim:

1. "Idle culling in the balance probes. The long-sims probes tick the whole world. Production
   culls idle mobs far from players (`idleMobTickRadius`). Turning culling on in the probes
   makes each one 6 to 9x faster: the lane drops from about 2,070 s to about 300 s per PR, and
   the nightly diet readers from about 7,900 s to about 1,100 s. The cost is that culling
   changes the rng stream, so every per-seed anchor has to be re-measured once at the culled
   config (values move by up to 20% per seed). The design-ratio bands stay as they are. The
   druid_bands nightly red (194.3 vs 191) would get re-banded as part of this. Adopt it?"
   Answered "Adopt and re-band (Recommended)".
2. "Scouring Mercy heal sanity bounds (v042_offense_packets). The bounds 2*130 to 2*155 assume
   a 2x crit, but heal crits are 1.5x, and gear Healing Power adds about 27, so a real crit
   spans about 236 to 273. The roll is now pinned (0.9), which reads 269. The decisive
   assertion is that the heal stays flat across Spell Power, and that one is sound. What should
   happen to the bounds?" Answered "Re-derive from formula (Recommended)".
3. "paladin_devotion_balance: the three rotation pins (holy, protection, retribution
   seconds-to-20-Devotion, pinned to 5 decimals) run on the full world's rng stream, and the
   file records about ten re-pins caused by far-world content moves. Moving them to
   EMPTY_TEST_WORLD saves about 15 s locally and ends that churn, at the cost of one re-pin now.
   Which do you want?" Answered "Empty world, re-pin once (Recommended)".
4. "Druid matrix nightly-only cells (druid_balance_probe): moongrove_3t and groveheart are 48 of
   the 96 nightly probes (8 seeds x 3 capstones x 2 profiles, about 1,500 s). They assert only
   that the best capstone's seed average is above zero, and more seeds weaken that check. As
   the class owner's stand-in, what do you want?" Answered "One seed, every row
   (Recommended)".

## How they were executed

The harness change landed first and alone (`49bb844579`): `scripts/probe_sim.ts` gives the
owned-class, druid, hunter and warlock harnesses one constructor, `newProbeSim`, which spreads
production's idle-mob cull last; a TypeScript-parser pin in `tests/idle_mob_tick_radius.test.ts`
holds the four harnesses to it (three mutants: a bare `new Sim(` in a harness, the caller's
radius winning the spread, a wrong radius; all killed). The rogue harness already runs on a
camp-free world and was left alone. Five agents then re-banded in their own worktrees off that
commit, under one brief: every band derived from a measurement re-pinned from its new actual
at its own depth with the relative margin its prior pin had over its prior recorded actual
(the unculled parent where none was recorded); design constants never move; one comment line
per re-pinned band; timeouts re-sized to about ten times the measured case, never below 60 s
nor above the old value; a must-pass control and a source mutant per re-pinned band through
the restore-verifying runner.

Judgment calls the groups flagged, kept as they are:
- Groveheart's group floors fell in absolute terms (0.45 to 0.307 of the lowest peer, 40 to 37
  hps) because the peer they are measured against rose under the cull (doctrine's three-ally
  run 105.7 to 142.1 hps); the relative margin is the prior one. A Groveheart-only 42 percent
  group cut now survives (the old floor at the unculled config would not have caught the same
  relative cut either). Groveheart also reads identically under all three capstones at seed
  4242: the every-row check reds a dead row but cannot tell the capstones apart there. For the
  class owner.
- The demonology five-minute end-pool rule (0.12) was never measured for demonology; it is
  the affliction rule copied, kept as a rule.
- The live-mob wildfang anchor moved 35 percent (5,315 to 7,165 damage): the rng trace re-rolls
  onto a 12-payoff row under the cull; seeds 42421 to 42425 land 5,749 to 7,096.

Ruling 2: the Scouring Mercy bounds are computed in the test from the ability's range, the
Healing Power rider and a literal 1.5x crit (the sim has only an inline literal, which importing
would move with a mutant): 236 to 273 against the pinned read of 269; a 2x and a 1.3x crit, a
Spell Power leak into the rider and a Doctrine-style heal multiplier are each killed (the last
only by the new ceiling).

Ruling 3: the three rotations run on `EMPTY_TEST_WORLD` (they were on a hub-yard-free custom
world, so no world-gated system changed); holy stays 41.25 s, protection 40.15 to 39.6,
retribution 55.75 to 46.85 (two Dawn's Wrath procs re-rolled), the 35 to 65 s band and the
blocking case's 29.65 s pin kept.

Ruling 4: one seed (4242, the seed the diet already runs and whose line of sight the banded
cells re-prove on every PR), every capstone row of moongrove_3t and groveheart above zero,
nightly-only. The nightly matrix fell from 96 probes to 54; a row forced to zero under either
profile fails the new case and passes the old best-above-zero check (17 of 17). Culled, the
saving is about 65 s of local nightly time, not the unculled estimate of 1,300 s.

The druid_bands nightly red reproduces unculled (194.32 against 191) and is gone at the culled
config (full actual 188.08, band re-derived). The DPS group took each band's margin over the
latest actual the file records for that depth; where that record was older than the parent,
some bands tightened sharply (the raid Thundercall over Vespers diet floor 0.66 to 0.94 against
a culled actual of 0.9535, about 1.4 percent of headroom; the Vespers area over single floor
1.25 to 1.37 and 1.36). The probes are deterministic and now culled, so far-world content no
longer re-rolls them, but a class change near these ratios will trip them first: for the class
owner. Bands the authoring commits state as design (the dps_probes tolerances, the naked-gear
15 percent, the Vespers over median 0.95) did not move.

All 17 lane files pass at diet depth on the integrated tip (82.5 s of wall, 640 s of test
bodies at the default local workers), and every group verified its files at full depth.
