# Freehold content name review

Reviewed by Codex on 2026-09-07 under
`src/sim/content/CLAUDE.md` Naming originality. This is a repository naming
review, not an assertion that a phrase has never appeared in another game.

The planned names are ordinary English descriptions: Timber Bed, Round Table,
Spindle Chair, Low Stool, Woven Rug, Brass Lantern, Storage Chest and Open
Bookshelf. Homesteader, Householder and Hearth are ordinary household words.
None introduces a coined fantasy word or a distinctive full character name.
The frozen IDs and planned names remain as specified in the content manifest.

Exact-phrase searches covered each furnishing name and both deed names, with
additional Homesteader/Householder searches scoped to Warcraft, RuneScape,
Final Fantasy XIV, Guild Wars 2, Elder Scrolls, Diablo and Path of Exile wikis.
RuneScape and the Diablo Fandom domain refused page access, so no page-inspection
claim is made for them. Search absence is not treated as proof of exclusivity.

Observed shared uses include Homesteader as a Guild Wars 2 title and an NPC role
in both Guild Wars 2 and Warcraft. The generic-English exception applies; the
new deed does not adopt their character names or lore.
[Guild Wars 2 title](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Guild_Wars_2_Online_Store),
[Homesteader Erika](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Homesteader_Erika),
[Wildhammer Homesteader](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Wildhammer_Homesteader).

Brass Lantern appears in Terraria, Woven Rug describes a Final Fantasy XIV
furnishing, and Storage Chest appears across unrelated games. These are direct
material, construction or function descriptions covered by the same exception.
[Terraria lanterns](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Snow_Lantern),
[Final Fantasy XIV Large Woven Rug](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/playguide/db/item/57782679fd7/),
[ARK Storage Chest](https://ark.wiki.gg/wiki/Mobile%3AStorage_Chest).

Verdict: retain the generic names. A future named furnisher, distinctive title,
or additional lore name still requires its own exact-phrase check before it is
authored. Art and numeric-source approval are separate gates.
