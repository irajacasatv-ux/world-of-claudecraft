Reverting the new open assignment to `this.nav = nav` and focus assignment to `nav` would leave the current empty-catalog test green. Add a small real-window case with the catalog dependency injected without Hearth, call `open('hearth')`, and pin no Hearth button/card, active Overview, and Overview focus. Do not mock the window or the navigation function.

2. **P2, high confidence: one of the four accepted decor-cost inputs is unexercised.** `tests/freehold_trial_geometry.test.ts:89` independently makes triangles, materials, and decoded bytes dominate, but never primitives. The producer is `scripts/freeholds/geometry_core.mjs:94`. Removing `primitives` from the component/max calculation would leave every current arithmetic assertion green. Add a primitives-only dominant fixture with a fresh literal cost (and keep the positive floor case). This matters because the accepted derivation explicitly uses the largest of all four ratios. Line numbers shifted from the original finding at test line 74 when source-sealing tests were added concurrently.

3. **P2, high confidence: indistinguishable observation fixtures cannot detect cross-alternative yield selection.** `tests/freehold_trial_economy.test.ts:105` gives every resource observation 52 base plus 3 fine, and `:149` only expects 55 for every line. Dropping `candidate.measuredIds.includes(row.alternativeId)` at `scripts/freeholds/economy_model.mjs:29` would select the first same-tier fish/produce observation for its sibling alternative and the suite would still pass. The runtime cycle suite compares frozen records to the sealed artifact, so it does not execute the proposal producer and cannot compensate. Give same-tier alternatives distinct yields and assert their independent selected quantities and source unit vectors. Add wrong/missing identity negatives while holding other match fields valid; protect family and material-tier matching independently, retaining the intentional hide/cloth tier reuse. These are dependency inputs to the actual producer, not a reimplementation of it.

No mutation was executed to prove these gaps because the coordinator explicitly reserved all checks. The counterfactuals above follow directly from the inspected assertions and called producers.
