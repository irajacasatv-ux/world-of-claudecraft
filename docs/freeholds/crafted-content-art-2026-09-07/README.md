# Crafted furnishing icon evidence

This batch owns the ten crafted furnishing paintings and their three recipe-document
paintings, identified by `targetSets.items` in `items.accepted-art.json`. These are
final item icons. Existing development model stand-ins and the final model approval
requirements belong to the separate accepted calibration; no GLB was generated here.

`staged-art-v2.json` retains the exact thirteen initial built-in generation prompts,
five targeted built-in edit prompts, ordered reference roles and hashes, original
and normalized master hashes, and canonical conversion evidence. Originals and
512px masters remain under ignored `tmp/imagegen/freehold-crafted-2026-09-07/`.
Only the 128px WebPs ship. `items.accepted-art.json` pins those shipping bytes and
the source evidence seal. The prior `staged-art.json` seal remains unchanged and
is explicitly superseded by the final source record. Runtime capture acceptance is
recorded separately in the accepted manifest.

The first rug painting was too wide for a circular slot. A first correction left
excessive ground; the final painting restores normal icon framing while keeping
the complete cloth silhouette safe. Each recipe document was refined with
its actual output painting as an identity reference, aligning the lamp mechanism,
easel supports and jewel setting. Earlier attempts remain in the source record.
No prior accepted asset batch was replaced or reattributed.

`master-review-1.webp` through `master-review-4.webp` follow the roster order and
show masters with size comparisons. The tall sheets can be resized by image viewers;
the original PNG masters were inspected individually at 512px. The native
`shipping-size-review-final.webp` has one row per item, with actual shipping art at 128px,
then 40px, 28px, 22px, 28px grayscale, and a 64px circular crop. Comparisons use the
viewed house anchors listed with hashes in the source record. These review images
are assembled inspection artifacts, not generated sheets. `rug-framing-final.webp`
shows the final rug master at 512px and its actual shipping size comparisons. The
earlier master sheets and `shipping-size-review.webp` remain as historical review
evidence; their rug rows are superseded by the final artifacts.

`naming-originality.json` records exact-phrase searches and the generic-vocabulary
verdicts for every item and First Hearth Crafts. Ordinary terms shared by unrelated
games remain permitted by the canonical naming rule; no external artwork or prose
was copied.

The coordinator owns actual runtime verification: all thirteen icons, the ten-output
Hearth page, the three quartermaster documents, and item names in English, Simplified
Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Russian. Relevant reused contexts
are bags, bank, vendor, tooltip, mail and valid action slots on desktop and touch
portrait/landscape. Furnishings and recipe documents are not equipment. Runtime
captures must use the final hashes in `items.accepted-art.json` and explicitly
distinguish injected inspection inventory from real acquisition actions.
